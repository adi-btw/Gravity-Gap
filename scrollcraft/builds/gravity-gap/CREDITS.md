All images are from the NASA Image and Video Library (images.nasa.gov), retrieved 2026-11-15 through
`https://images-api.nasa.gov`. Each item's search record and `metadata.json` were checked for a non-NASA copyright
or usage notice; none was found (the only "copyright" fields present are the embedded Hewlett-Packard sRGB ICC colour
profile string, which is not an image rights notice). NASA imagery is generally not subject to copyright in the
United States; NASA's media usage guidelines ask that NASA be credited and that NASA insignia or logos not be used to
imply endorsement. The page uses no NASA insignia or logos (the logo and text baked into GRC-1998-C-00486 were cropped
off), and states: "Gravity Gap is an independent NASA Space Apps 2026 project. It is not a NASA product."

Files are resized and re-encoded (WebP) with Pillow; no content was generated or altered beyond cropping and the
cover's alpha cut (the sky is keyed out and the lunar surface separated along the measured horizon).
